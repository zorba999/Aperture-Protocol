Ground Truth: our new brand campaign

advertiser: Meridian Bank
campaign: Ground Truth
first air date: national television, all networks
flight: six months
secondary: paid social cutdowns, Europe wide
agency: Northwind Creative

Ground Truth is the largest brand campaign Meridian Bank has run. The film opens on an aerial descent over the Icelandic ice cap before moving to the customer stories at the centre of the work.

The spot begins its national television flight this month and runs for six months, supported by paid social cutdowns across our European markets.
